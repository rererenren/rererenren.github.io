1. Warm, layered lighting

Overhead lights kill the cozy vibe. Instead, layer soft, warm light sources: a desk lamp with a warm bulb (2700K–3000K), a small string of fairy lights, and maybe a candle (real or LED). The goal is pools of gentle light, not one harsh beam.

  • Warm-white desk lamp angled to avoid screen glare
  • Ambient accent light behind the monitor (reduces eye strain, too)
  • Dim the room so the warm spots stand out

2. A clutter-free desk layout

Lo-fi study spaces feel calm because they're uncluttered. Keep only what you use daily on the desk: laptop, a notebook, a warm drink, maybe one plant. Everything else gets a home off the desk.

  • One warm drink = instant cozy anchor
  • A notebook + pen for analog thinking breaks
  • A small plant or two for life and softness
